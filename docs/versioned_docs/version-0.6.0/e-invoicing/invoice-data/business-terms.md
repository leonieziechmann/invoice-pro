# Business Terms

EN 16931 describes an invoice by its business terms (BT) and business groups (BG), such as the invoice number (BT-1) or the seller (BG-4). The tables below list each of them with what `invoice-pro` does with it:

- **input:** an input of `invoice-pro` states it, and _Details_ names the input: a parameter of `invoice`, a key of a party, a component or one of its arguments.
- **derived:** `invoice-pro` derives it, and _Details_ says from what.
- **not supported:** `invoice-pro` does not state it, and _Details_ says why.

The profile decides which of them the XML states (see [What Each Profile States](../index.md#what-each-profile-states)); an input the profile cannot state is reported as a warning (`IP-PROFILE-01`). The scheme identifiers of identifiers (BT-29-1, BT-30-1, BT-34-1, ...) share the entry of their business term. The tables are generated from `tools/zugferd/bt-disposition.toml` of the repository, which the tests keep complete: every term has a decision.

[//]: # "business-term tables: generated by tools/zugferd/bt_disposition.py --update-docs"

Of the 196 business terms and groups of EN 16931, `invoice-pro` states 129 from an input, derives 30 and does not support 37.
